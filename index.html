Questions

Before you enquire

Do you have cars in stock?

No. Every car is sourced to order, so you get the colour, trim and mileage you want rather than whatever is on the forecourt. Sourcing usually takes one to three weeks once your deposit is agreed.

Is insurance included?

No. You arrange hire-and-reward insurance in your own name, which keeps your no-claims history with you. We need to see the policy before handover.

What happens if I need to stop early?

The agreement sets out a hand-back process and any charges in plain terms. We go through it with you before you sign.

Can I choose the colour?

Yes. Black is the most requested for executive work, and we'll search for it first if that's what you want.
